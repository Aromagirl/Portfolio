## Tickeron responsive website

Tickeron is a web-based, interactive Financial Marketplace. The company has developed state-of-the-art Artificial Intelligence (A.I.) that can optimize portfolios based on asset allocation and stock selection. Additionally it can also identify dozens of technical trading patterns for thousands of stocks. Their A.I. can be used to generate investment ideas or to recommend changes to investment strategies.

For investors and those interested in learning more about investing in the stock market, the Tickeron website provides endless resources. Users can access (and create) webcasts, newsletters, investment clubs/groups, informative blog posts, and a comprehensive library of investment terms.

For advisors and thought leaders in the investment community, Tickeron provides a platform on which you can share, broadcast, and/or sell your ideas. You can connect with clients or users seeking advice and use online tools to manage your relationships.

[Tickeron](https://tickeron.com/)
