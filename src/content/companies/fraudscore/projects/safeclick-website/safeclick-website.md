## About SafeClick & my role

SafeClick helps advertisers understand the quality of their website traffic and identify invalid clicks. Its website introduces the product, explains its capabilities, and lets visitors explore pricing and request early access.

The redesign brings the key pages into a consistent visual system, replacing the previous blue and purple direction with dark backgrounds, mint accents, stronger typography, and unified cards and buttons.

The scope includes the desktop and mobile homepage, Pricing, Early access, and Glossary, alongside Product comparison and Design Partners pages that support product evaluation.

## My role as Product Designer:

- 🔍   Analysis of existing design and architecture
- 🖌️   Creating company style
- 🦼   Accounting for accessibility
- 📱   Responsive design
- 🔄   Iterating on designs
- 🤖   Development with the involvement of AI tools
- 🚀   Testing and Launch
