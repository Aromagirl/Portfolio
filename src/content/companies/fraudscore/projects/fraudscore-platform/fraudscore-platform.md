## About FraudScore Platform & my role

<div class="tickeron-ui-intro">
<div class="tickeron-ui-description">
<p>FraudScore helps advertisers and advertising platforms analyse traffic quality and identify advertising fraud. The platform brings together reports, integration controls, monitoring and account administration.</p>
<p>My work covered interface structure, detailed screen layouts and interaction states, supported by connected prototypes and shared UI guidelines. The examples below show how the product handles dense data, configuration and access permissions.</p>
</div>
<section class="tickeron-ui-responsibilities" aria-labelledby="platform-role">
<h3 id="platform-role">My role as Product Designer:</h3>
<ul>
<li>🔍 Analysis of existing design and architecture</li>
<li>💡 Defining screen structure and user flows</li>
<li>🦼 Accounting for accessibility</li>
<li>📱 Responsive design</li>
<li>🎨 Designing platform screens and interaction states</li>
<li>🔗 High-fidelity prototyping</li>
<li>📚 Defining UI foundations and components</li>
<li>🔄 Iterating on designs</li>
<li>🤝 Collaborating with the development team</li>
</ul>
</section>
</div>
