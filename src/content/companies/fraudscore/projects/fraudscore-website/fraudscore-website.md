## About FraudScore & my role

FraudScore is an independent ad fraud detection and prevention solution. Its website helps visitors explore the platform, compare plans, and learn about advertising fraud.

The redesign connects product and educational content through a consistent visual language: deep teal backgrounds, cyan accents, larger typography, and structured cards. Updated layouts give product capabilities and calls to action a clearer hierarchy.

The scope includes the desktop and mobile homepage, Pricing, Event Analytics, Blog, and article pages. The calculator, industry page, and glossary provide additional ways to explore the product and its use cases.
