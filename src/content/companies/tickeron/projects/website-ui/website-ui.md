## About Tickeron & my role

<div class="tickeron-ui-intro">
<div class="tickeron-ui-description">
<p>Tickeron is an investment platform combining AI-powered market analysis with a community of investors and financial professionals. Members can explore investment content, discover portfolios and share their expertise.</p>
<p>My work focused on responsive interfaces for the platform: newsfeed and article layouts, marketplace and portfolio filters, member profiles, membership levels and support pages.</p>
<p>The designs cover desktop, tablet and mobile layouts, with high-fidelity prototypes showing navigation, filtering and profile editing states.</p>
</div>
<section class="tickeron-ui-responsibilities" aria-labelledby="tickeron-ui-role">
<h3 id="tickeron-ui-role">My role as UI Designer:</h3>
<ul>
<li>🎨 Designing mockups for platform features</li>
<li>📱 Responsive design for desktop, tablet and mobile</li>
<li>🔗 High-fidelity prototyping</li>
<li>🤝 Collaborating with the development team</li>
</ul>
</section>
