Mafia Game brings the classic social deduction game to Telegram. A bot helps a group get started and follow the game, while a Mini App provides a dedicated interface for the lobby, player roles and voting.

The concept focuses on making group play easy to understand: creating a room, inviting friends, joining a session and knowing what to do next. Clear game states and timely prompts help players follow the action, while private role information keeps the mystery intact.

The design connects two experiences: quick actions and notifications in the chat, and a focused game interface inside the Mini App. Special attention goes to first-time players, readable instructions and a comfortable mobile experience.

## My role as Product Designer:

- 🗺️ Mapping player journeys and game flows
- 💡 Designing wireframes for the bot and Mini App
- 🔗 Low and high-fidelity prototyping
- 🎨 Creating a consistent visual language and interface components
- ♿ Accounting for accessibility and clear game states
- 🤝 Collaborating with the development team
- 🔄 Iterating on designs
